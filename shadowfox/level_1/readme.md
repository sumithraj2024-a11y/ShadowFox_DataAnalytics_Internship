Sample Superstore: Sales & Profit Analysis

Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the ShadowFox Data Analytics Internship – Beginner Level.

The objective of this project is to analyse the Sample Superstore dataset and understand sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer segments, regions, and shipping methods using Microsoft Excel.

Dataset

The Sample Superstore dataset contains sales transaction records with information about orders, customers, products, sales, profit, quantity, discounts, and regions.

- 9,994 transaction records
- 21 columns
- Sales and profit-related business information

Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- Charts
- Excel Dashboard
- Data Analysis & Visualization

Analysis Performed

1. Data Exploration

- Examined the dataset structure and business-related fields.
- Checked for duplicate records.
- Checked for missing values.
- Reviewed sales, profit, quantity, and discount information.

2. Sales & Profit Analysis

- Analysed total sales revenue.
- Analysed total profit and quantity sold.
- Calculated overall profit margin.
- Examined the relationship between sales and profitability.

3. Regional Analysis

- Compared sales performance across different regions.
- Identified the highest and lowest revenue-generating regions.
- Analysed regional contribution to overall sales.

4. Category Analysis

- Compared sales across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
- Identified the highest-performing product category.
- Analysed category-level contribution to sales.

5. Customer Segment Analysis

- Compared Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- Identified the segment contributing the highest sales.
- Analysed segment-level sales performance.

6. Shipping Analysis

- Compared sales across different shipping modes.
- Identified the most commonly used shipping method.
- Analysed the contribution of each shipping mode to sales.

7. Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard was created using PivotTables and charts to provide a clear overview of:

- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity
- Profit Margin
- Regional performance
- Category performance
- Customer segment performance
- Shipping performance

Key Findings

- Total Sales Revenue: ₹2,297,200.86
- Total Profit: ₹286,397
- Total Quantity Sold: 37,873
- Overall Profit Margin: 12.47%
- West region recorded the highest sales.
- Technology was the highest-performing category by sales.
- Consumer was the highest-contributing customer segment.
- Standard Class was the most widely used shipping mode.

Business Insights

The analysis shows that sales performance varies across regions, categories, customer segments, and shipping methods. Technology generated the highest category-level sales, while the West region contributed the highest regional sales.

The analysis also highlights the importance of monitoring profitability alongside sales, as high sales do not always guarantee high profit.

Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing product categories while monitoring their profitability.
- Strengthen sales strategies in lower-performing regions.
- Develop targeted offers for high-value customer segments.
- Monitor discounts carefully to maintain healthy profit margins.
- Ensure sufficient inventory for consistently high-demand products.
- Use dashboard-based monitoring for regular business performance tracking.
